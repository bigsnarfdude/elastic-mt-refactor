# Sprint S2 — Nucleation Unification · RESULTS (2026-07-05)

Prereg: `PREREG_S2_nucleation_unification.md`. Ran same day.

## Verdict: hypothesis CONFIRMED — the gap was nucleation flux.

Matching the **areal nucleation flux** (2e-4 /µm²/s, isotropic, zero interaction) collapses the
CorticalSim↔Cytosim reference-density gap from **5.4× → 1.12×** — inside the pre-registered 1.5× success band.

| engine | condition | areal flux (/µm²/s) | density (µm/µm²) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cytosim | nonsteric (reused anchor) | 2e-4 (0.5/s ÷ 2500 µm²) | **3.83** |
| CorticalSim | nozipper, kNuc=0.0002, n=5 | **2.00e-4 realized** | **4.30 ± 0.05** |
| — before matching (v0.2) | CorticalSim kNuc=0.001 | 1e-3 | 20.80 (=5.4× Cytosim) |

**Ratio at matched flux = 1.12×.** For reference the pure-arithmetic prediction was 20.80/5 = 4.16; measured 4.30.

## Why this is trustworthy (guardrails from the prereg, all cleared)
1. **Realized flux verified, not just the knob.** Measured from CorticalSim's own `nucleation_ev` column:
   2.00e-4 /µm²/s (target 2.0e-4). Cross-checked mid-run: at t=480, nucleation_ev=1754 vs predicted
   0.0002 × 18221 µm² × 480 s = 1749. The two engines are genuinely at the same flux.
2. **Areal density convention (bench's), not S1's region-sum.** CorticalSim reports density natively in
   µm/µm² in `measurements.txt` col 2; Cytosim anchor is the same µm/µm² from v0.2. Not the S1 ~1642 number.
3. **Both references are genuine zero-interaction, isotropic.** CorticalSim nozipper config: `zippering 0`,
   `ind_cat 0`, `kCross 0`, `induced_cat_fraction 0`, `nucleationType isotropic`. Cytosim nonsteric = no
   steric force. DI params match the spec in both (v_g 0.08, v_s -0.16, r_c 0.003, r_r 0.005).
4. **Matched DOWN to Cytosim** (2e-4), so no thousands-of-MTs Cytosim re-run; anchor reused after verifying
   its config. Feasible in one morning.

## Honest caveats
- **Density still mildly ramping at t=1800.** Last-50% mean (v0.2's convention, apples-to-apples) = 4.30;
  the final-point value is ~5.48. Even the conservative final value gives 5.48/3.83 = 1.43× — still within
  1.5×. Both readings pass. A longer run would tighten the steady-state estimate.
- **Two engines, not three.** This resolves the *named* gap (Cytosim ↔ CorticalSim, the 5.4×). elastic-mt is
  a third point but its nucleation is in tangled scaled units (`r_n0·R³/v_g`, rescaled by `accept_MT`), so
  matching it needs a measure-and-tune calibration loop — deliberately left as the stretch, not ballooned in.

## What it means for the collaboration
Standardizing nucleation flux is not just advisable, it is *sufficient* to bring two very different engines'
zero-interaction reference densities into agreement (5.4×→1.1×). This directly upgrades the June caveat
("nucleation probably needs standardizing before any cross-engine density claim") into a demonstrated result:
here is exactly what standardizing it buys. It unblocks cross-engine density comparison — the prerequisite S2
was meant to clear.
